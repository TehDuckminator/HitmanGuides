Verified against the in-game challenge export you provided

  • The real total is 58, not the 60/61/83 that circulate online. Your export lists 15 Assassination, 22 Discovery, 14 Feats, 2 Targets, 5 Classics = 58. The tracker matches exactly.
  • The three escalation challenges are correctly excluded. The Phoenix Ascension and both Asmodeus Waltz tiers are separate escalation contracts, not part of this mission's 58 — they are not in your export, so they are not tracked here. This was the source of every inflated count.
  • Several conditions were corrected to match the official wording. Icarus is an electrocution at the sun installation, not a generic accident. Impactful Art requires both targets under a single chandelier. Conserving Ammunition specifically requires a sniper rifle. Geronimo fires when you trigger the evacuation with the keycard readers, not on the parachute kill. Here Goes Nothing is an escape via parachute. Cashing Out is a pacify, not a kill.
  • Flying Monkey Business is [REDACTED] in the game data. The official description is hidden, so the method shown (banana-peel slip during the evacuation) is community-sourced and labelled as such rather than presented as official.

What genuinely cannot be verified

  • Guard names and patrol timings. Dubai's guards are unnamed generic NPCs across every source. Named NPCs, all confirmed: Carl Ingram, Marcus Stuyvesant, Cordelia Stuyvesant, Lucy (the artist), Zana "The Vulture" Kazem, Hans Lucht, Omar Al-Ghazali, the Sheikh.
  • Per-challenge XP is only partially published. Where a source states a figure it is shown (Icarus and Angry Birdy are each 4,000 XP, confirmed by two guides each). For every other challenge no reliable figure exists, and it is marked so rather than invented.

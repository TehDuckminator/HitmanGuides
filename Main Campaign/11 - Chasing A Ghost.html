Hitman: World of Assassination · Mumbai

Chasing a Ghost v6 · STEP-BY-STEP

80 challenges · 8 validated runs. Every step names where to go, the disguise, the item, the NPC, the action, and any missable warning.

How to read this

Each step is laid out the same way — GO (where), DISGUISE, ITEM, NPC, DO (the action) — plus a warning line when something is missable. Challenge names and objectives come from the mission's own data file; the tactical geography (rooms, items, NPCs) is drawn from the standard Mumbai walkthrough. Anything genuinely random (the Maelstrom's face, alt-start unlocks) is called out rather than invented.

Coins matter: phone booths and the cloth vendor need coins. Bring 3–4 in your loadout; more sit by shop cash registers and in the tailor shop.

Four rules behind the routing. (1) Challenges are permanent — you never lose one by doing runs in order. (2) The Maelstrom's face is random each replay, so any run that kills him identifies him first. (3) You can't kill one target two ways in a single run — save before the kill, reload per variant. (4) Metas (Versatile Assassin, The Helping Hand, The Classics) tick off cumulatively.

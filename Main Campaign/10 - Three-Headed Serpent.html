Corrections this build applies (from the authoritative data)

It's 65 challenges, not 66. The uploaded file lists 25 Assassination, 6 Discovery, 26 Feats, 3 Targets, 5 Classic. There is no Codename 47, no "Mission Stories - Santa Fortuna" aggregate, and no "The Hero of Santa Fortuna" in this (World of Assassination) version.

Several challenges were misdescribed in my earlier guides and are now corrected to the official text: Still as a Statue kills Rico (not Andrea). Blowing Bubbles drowns Catalina Delgado in her hot tub (a non-target). Plain Sailing / The Wind in Your Hair / Pop a Wheelie / Low Mileage are all ESCAPE challenges (speed boat / Rico's sports car / motorbike / local's car) — not stealth, tattoo, run-over, or no-vehicle challenges. Authentic Local Cuisine is a poisoned-street-food KILL, not eating food. It Looks Nothing like Her is gaining mansion access as the tattooist (a feat), not a kill. Drown Your Sorrows is listening to P-Power. Cog in the Machine / Brick and Mortar are collection feats. Nature Finds a Way pacifies 5 coca-field guards; The Healing eliminates 10 as the Shaman. Tasteless, Traceless IS a Santa Fortuna challenge (I wrongly excluded it before).

Four escapes are mutually exclusive (one exit per run), so they're spread across Runs 2, 4, 5, 6.

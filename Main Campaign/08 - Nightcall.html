Six nights · the house is empty until the office computer runs

Four rules that govern every run

  • There are no Mission Stories. The wiki states this outright and your challenge data contains none. Nothing here can be broken by story-script ordering, and no "Hero of Hawke's Bay" challenge exists.
  • The map is empty until the office computer is used. No guards, no target, no witnesses. Every pickup and every discovery area in this guide is collected before that moment, in every run.
  • The lockpick comes before the poison. The lockpick sits in the Panic Room; the lethal poison sits in a locked cabinet in the second-floor bathroom. Runs 4 and 5 both open the Panic Room first for exactly this reason.
  • Alertness never resets. Once anyone in the level is alerted, Alma's bodyguards stay on high alert for the rest of the attempt. This is why every run front-loads its quiet objectives.

Run the runs in order

Runs 1–3 are the mastery engine. The Office starting location only unlocks at Mastery 5, which is this destination's cap, so a fresh profile will be starting from the Boat for the first few attempts. Runs 4, 5 and 6 assume you have Office by then; if not, every one of them opens with the same Boat approach used in Run 2, which costs about ninety seconds.

One dependency is hard: Versatile Assassin needs Straight Shot, which is earned in Run 2. Do Run 2 before Run 4 or it will not fire. The Classics needs Sniper Assassin from Run 3 plus the three from Run 2.

The house, room by room

One beach house, a garage, a pool room and a shoreline. Where sources disagree, both readings are given rather than one being picked.

The six discovery areas

These are the exact area names from your challenge data, and there are exactly six of them. Older web guides list a "ruined boathouse" as a separate area — it is not one. The Garage is the one people miss, because the obvious routes into the house go through the pool room or the front door.

  • Boat / House — the shoreline, the grounds and the main floor, all counted as a single area.
  • Garage — enter by shorting the signal box with the screwdriver, or through the Panic Room's second door.
  • Alma's Office — second floor, straight ahead from the top of the garage stairs.
  • Alma's / Bedroom — second floor, off the hallway.
  • Roof — via the bathroom balcony or the drainpipe beside the garage door.
  • Panic Room — behind the square painting on the office wall.

Named NPCs

Three people in this level have names in any verifiable source. Everyone else is described by post, because inventing names would be worse than admitting the gap.

  • Alma Reynard — the only target. Does not exist on the map until the computer is used.
  • Orson Mills — her partner, ex-CICADA. The level's only non-combatant civilian, and the subject of two Feats. Killing him voids "only eliminate the target" on every Classic.
  • Gerard Mann — Head of Security, personal bodyguard to Reynard and Mills. He enters through the front door beside Alma.

The wiki records 34 non-target NPCs, of whom Orson is the only civilian. Mercenaries appear on the second floor after the trigger, and the wiki walkthrough notes they are lax enough that even when ordered to sweep upstairs they do not check the office or the arms room.

Scripted behaviour worth knowing

  • The Panic Room is one-shot. During an emergency Alma and Orson lock themselves in, and it can then be opened from neither the office painting nor the garage door. If it is breached or discovered, she instead runs into the sea and drowns herself. Reported variants: she huddles by the mercenaries at the grave-digging truck, or stops at the water's edge and stays alive. In all cases the Panic Room script is gone for that attempt.
  • Bedtime has a timer. If undisturbed, Alma wakes roughly fifteen minutes after going to sleep to phone her sister Celia. Sleeping-target challenges have to land inside that window.
  • Being sick is not an alert. Emetic poison sends an NPC to a toilet without putting anyone into an alert state. It works on Orson as well as Alma, which is a clean way to separate the pair.
  • The tea choice is random. Alma picks either sugar or honey. The wiki walkthrough's answer is to poison both. A Steam guide instead describes hiding behind the kitchen counter and poisoning the chosen ingredient in the moment while the tea is being made.

Patch history

  • HITMAN 2 patch 2.21, Shinobi pack: added Waterbender, Deadly Ninja, Deadly Shadow, Contained Explosion. The "Shinobi" meta-challenge that unlocked the Tanto was cut in HITMAN III; the four survive as Assassination challenges.
  • HITMAN 2 patch 2.71.0, Thriller Night pack: added Vertigo, The Scissor Man, Hitman with a Shotgun and Hitman of the Black Lagoon. Black Lagoon and the "Thriller Night" meta-challenge were cut in HITMAN III; the other three survive.
  • Also cut in HITMAN III: the classic challenge No Evidence. Guides quoting 34+ challenges for this mission are counting cut content; guides quoting 43 are counting the whole Hawke's Bay destination including escalations.

Still unverified or conflicting

  • Emetic rat poison locations — the wiki walkthrough says storage room and downstairs bathroom; a PSN trophy guide describes a shelf near the aquarium. All three are worth a sweep.
  • Who makes the tea — one guide says Orson prepares it and asks Alma which she wants; another describes a guard doing it. The mechanic is the same either way.
  • Hitman with a Shotgun weapon restriction — several users in one Steam thread report it only counting with a shotgun found in the level, never a loadout one, and report the counter resetting for no visible reason. Community-reported, never confirmed by IOI. The guide uses the Panic Room arsenal shotgun as a precaution.
  • Vertigo reliability — reported as inconsistent, especially in Contract Mode. Confirmed working by two separate users: pushing Alma off the second-floor balcony while she smokes, and dumping a body from the roof above the fireplace.
  • Guard captain disguise — one guide claims the captain beside Alma on arrival does not yield the standard outfit. Single source; simply use another guard.
  • Deadly Ninja persistence — no source states whether the ten kills must happen in one attempt. Treated as single-attempt, which is safe either way.
  • Contained Explosion isolation window — no published guide names a moment when Alma is provably alone. The route given (emetic poison sends her alone to the ground-floor bathroom, where a proximity charge is waiting) is derived from two separately sourced behaviours rather than lifted from a guide, and is labelled as such in the step.
  • Full NPC patrol routes — UNVERIFIED. No source documents them. Only positions that sources actually describe are named: the guard at the pool door, a second guard who sometimes crosses the pool area, the guard at the foot of the drainpipe, the two roof guards, the mercenaries around the boat, the crew at the grave-digging truck, and the bodyguard who walks into the bedroom shortly after Alma arrives.

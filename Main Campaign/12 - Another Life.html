Hard rules that must be obeyed or challenges become impossible:
  • Cameras before recorders. For You Had Me at Bodyguard and the sniper challenges, destroy the 12 cameras — destroying the recordings first stops the count.
  • Janus can only die once. Every Janus kill method (Finger on the Pulse, Tea Time, Monster Cookie, Memorabilia, Death from Below, Sneezing Suspicion, electric, sniper) is mutually exclusive within a single run. Same for Cassidy.
  • SASO needs the default start. Only the default starting location spawns you in the suit — no other start can earn Silent Assassin, Suit Only.
  • Elderly Assistance needs Janus alive through all 7 sub-tasks — do not kill him until it completes.
  • Doing the Rounds: all five newspapers must sit in mailboxes at the same time. The jogger empties mailboxes she passes — pacify her first or stay behind her.

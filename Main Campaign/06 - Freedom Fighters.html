Run-logic validation (simulated step by step)

  • 60 of 62 challenges are assigned. The two left out are Chameleon and Discover Colorado.
  • All four targets die in every run. Colorado does not let you leave one alive, so each run below eliminates Sean, Maya, Ezra and Penelope and then exits.
  • Prerequisites precede use. Run 1 takes the rat poison before the technician and the Interpol badge before Penelope; Run 2 takes the wrench and loosens the valve before Maya arrives; Run 5 stages the weapon that lures the cook before it needs his outfit; Runs 6 and 7 take the stashed coins out of the laundry room early.
  • Nothing needed later is destroyed earlier. Every run's kills are ordered so that a target still needed for a later challenge in the same run is not killed first — Run 3 keeps Ezra alive until after the drugs are administered, and Run 4 keeps Sean and Penelope alive until they are standing close enough for the watch to take both.
  • Un-spotted requirements are isolated. Run 3 carries They Scream, They Cry, which is the opposite — it requires being spotted — and no other no-detection challenge shares that run.
  • The Classics capstone lands last. Run 8 delivers Sniper Assassin; Run 9 delivers Silent Assassin, Silent Assassin Suit Only and Suit Only, and the capstone fires on Run 9's completion.

Known conflicts, bugs and ordering rules

  • Run 4, Ezra can freeze. fish reports a bug during testing where Ezra stopped moving at that point in the run. Stand next to him until his dialogue with another NPC triggers and he resumes.
  • Run 4, grab the fallen weapons. Pacified NPCs' guns sometimes clip into the floor. Pick them up anyway — leaving weapons lying about starts investigations.
  • Run 3, ignore the cameras when lacing the cigarettes. fish says explicitly to accept being seen by the cameras there; the evidence is destroyed later in the same run.
  • Run 5, the scarecrow burns people. Once Wicker Man fires, NPCs burst into flames as you walk past them. That is the intended effect and is what makes the body-count challenges in that run easy — but it also means you cannot walk anywhere quietly afterwards.
  • Run 5, dump bodies before they are bagged. A target's body must reach the slurry pit before the militia bag it, so kill each target near the pit: Penelope and Maya are already close, and Ezra is close when he walks to the chemistry shed.
  • The whole map is hostile territory. There is no neutral ground anywhere in Colorado, and the only camera recorder is on the ground floor of the farmhouse. On a suit-only run, cameras must be shot on sight rather than cleaned up afterwards.
  • Difficulty: the branch-and-reload structure assumes unlimited manual saves, i.e. Casual. The Classic challenges are no longer difficulty-tiered in World of Assassination, so Casual costs nothing — though NamuWiki notes the map still plays like the old Professional difficulty because every zone is hostile regardless.

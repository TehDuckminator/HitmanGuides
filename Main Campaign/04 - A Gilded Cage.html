Run-logic validation (simulated step by step)

  • 58 of 62 challenges are assigned. The four left out are Seven Years of Bad Luck, Pipe Dreams, Chameleon and Discover Marrakesh — see above.
  • Prerequisites precede use. Run 1 takes the wrench before the headmaster and the officer outfit before the cell; Run 2 takes the keycard before the double doors and the coins before the fountain; Run 5 takes both safe codes and the car key before either summons; Run 6 takes the truck key before the turret.
  • Nothing needed later is destroyed earlier. Run 1 does the toilet drop behind a save and reloads, because Zaydan is needed alive for Personal Vendetta. Run 4 does the dance kill behind a save and reloads, because Zaydan is needed alive for the oil lamp.
  • Un-spotted requirements are isolated. Run 1 carries both New Transfer and Uninvited, and they are separated by a long section with two saves between them. No other run carries a no-detection challenge.
  • Both targets die every run. Every run below eliminates Strandberg and Zaydan and exits, so nothing is left half-finished and every mission-completion challenge in the run actually banks.
  • The Classics capstone lands last. Run 7 delivers Silent Assassin, Silent Assassin Suit Only and Suit Only; Run 8 delivers Sniper Assassin and fires the capstone.

Known conflicts, bugs and ordering rules

  • Run 4 needs all nine soldiers dismissed. The radio only spawns in the first-floor office once every one of the nine patrolling elite soldiers has been dismissed — four on the ground floor, five upstairs. Miss one and Let's Dance is unreachable that run.
  • Run 3, the protester's timing drifts. fish reports that the protester drinks his water at different moments even when everything before it is identical, and believes it is tied to standing close enough to hear his speech. Watch him rather than counting seconds; the news crew waits for you regardless.
  • Run 3, get into the shisha stall fast. The stall left of the bar has to be poisoned before the enforcer spots you and before the cameraman walks in. It is a narrow window at the very start of the run.
  • Run 1, crouch to poison the intern's drink. Standing while spiking the reception water bottle raises suspicion; crouching does not.
  • Run 2, park the two consulate-square NPCs. Trigger the dialogue of the two people by the car before you go for the protester, so they are out of the way later.
  • Difficulty: the branch-and-reload structure assumes unlimited manual saves, i.e. Casual. The Classic challenges are no longer difficulty-tiered in World of Assassination.

Berlin has no Mission Stories. The wiki states this outright. So there is no story order to protect, no "Hero of Berlin" challenge, and no story-vs-story script conflict. Everything here is opportunity-driven.
The rule that governs every run. The instant your fifth ICA agent dies, the survivors lose target status and flee north/west. Any challenge needing a specific agent alive must be done before kill five. Save before that kill, every time. Challenge progress banks across reloads while you are online — do a challenge, reload, do the next, then finish the mission.

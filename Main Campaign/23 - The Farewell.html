Corrections made to v1 of this guide

Deeper sourcing (Gamepressure's per-target assassination pages, which v1 didn't have) contradicted several v1 steps. Those are fixed here:

  • The Talented Mr. Rieper is an ambush, not a safe isolation. v1 recommended it as the cleanest suit-only fiber-wire opportunity on Vidal. Wrong — mercenaries are waiting in the Cinema and attack. It is now a save-then-reload step, and the SASO route no longer uses it.
  • Pencil Pusher — wrong location. v1 had you intercept the lawyer in the Parking Lot. Gamepressure explicitly calls that an unnecessary risk: wait for him to occupy the Villa Guest Room instead, and the Research File is already on the desk there.
  • Firebrand is in the Asado, not "around the guest areas", and the documented trigger is a screwdriver-sabotaged lamp plus a faucet, not shooting the lamp.
  • Piano Man / Straight Shot have a specific spot: Yates stops at the fireplace in the Villa Foyer with a single mercenary present. v1 said vaguely "the upper floor".
  • Hold My Hair is far easier on Vidal (emetic Pinot in the Tasting Room → she uses the Level 2 bathroom) and now stacks with Notes of Hemlock off one save.
  • Secondary Fermentation needs a Security Keycard staged in advance — the Security Room is locked. v1 sent you there empty-handed.
  • Grand Theft Auto is free at the end of Closing Statement — the car keys are in the same bedroom the story ends in. v1 spent a separate run on it.

Bugs and hard ordering rules

  • Eyes On Target "Mission Story Missed" bug. The snipers kill Vidal but the story logs as failed. Reported cause, attributed to an IOI developer post: saving and reloading after you camera-scan Vidal. Do not save between the scan and the kill order. A fix was slated for the late-Feb-2021 patch; current status in World of Assassination is unverified.
  • The Tour must finish before Closing Statement exists. Closing Statement has no menu entry. It is added to your log only when Yates, at the end of the tour, tells his guard to fetch wine from the sommelier. Kill either target before that line and Closing Statement, The Hidden Hand, A Vintage Year, the Sommelier and Head of Security disguises and 47's Signature Suit are all gone for that timeline.
  • The Orator ordering. Swap the microphone and raise the voltage regulator before lighting the asado pit. Light the fire first and Yates can reach the stage before the trap is armed. Also don't drop or throw the torch in public after use.
  • Cuckoo's Nest vs Eyes On Target. Stealing the silo team's rifle removes the weapon the snipers need to shoot Vidal. Reveal the story first, then branch: rifle theft on one save, the sniper kill order on another.
  • Melon Head needs the sniper team alive and functional. Don't pacify them on the Eyes On Target branch.
  • Shortcuts open from one side only. All three Mendoza shortcuts are locked doors that cannot be opened from the front even with keys, a lockpick or a crowbar. Each must be reached from the opposite side. Backdoor and Maintenance Door: approach from the north.
  • Coldhearted kills a civilian. Sealing Vidal in the freezer also kills Gabriel Vargas. Diana berates you for it and it breaks Silent Assassin. Fine in Run 2, fatal in Run 6.
  • Discover Mendoza counter bug. Players reported the map showing all areas revealed while the counter stuck at 42/45. Historical (2021), current status unverified. If it happens, re-enter Wine Fridge, Villa Attic and Backside.
  • Pulp Friction physics quirk. The body sometimes bounces back onto the crusher rim. Cosmetic — the wiki confirms the challenge still awards.
  • Diana cannot be permanently subdued. Sedative gas drops her but she wakes as soon as the ventilation is off, while everyone else stays down. Plan around her.

Mutually exclusive challenges

Each target dies once per timeline, so every kill-method challenge for the same target competes with every other. The runs below separate them with manual saves; challenge progress is written to your profile the moment it pops and survives a reload, so only the last kill of a run needs to reach an exit.

  • Vidal methods (one per timeline): Notes of Hemlock · Hold My Hair · Firebrand · Pulp Friction · Coldhearted · A Thick Must · Eyes On Target/Murder by Proxy · Brother from another Brother · Piano Man · Secondary Fermentation · the SASO propane accident.
  • Yates methods (one per timeline): Domestic Disturbance · The Orator · Pencil Pusher · Get Rid of the Body · To Loyalty · Closing Statement's knife finish · Over The Top's electrocution · Straight Shot · Piano Man · Secondary Fermentation · Brother from another Brother.
  • A Fine Vintage forbids any outfit change — it cannot share a run with any Chameleon pickup, The Hidden Hand, or any disguise-gated area.
  • Sniper Assassin requires both targets shot with a sniper rifle, locking out every other kill challenge that run.
  • Silent Assassin, Suit Only locks out all 15 disguises and all four disguise Discovery challenges.
  • Secondary Fermentation consumes both targets at once and cannot combine with any other kill challenge.
  • To Loyalty kills Yates mid-meeting and ends the Closing Statement chain early — it must be a save-branch, never the main line.

Route validation — what was checked

  • Prerequisites before use. Every tool is picked up before the step that needs it: screwdriver and wrench before the Over The Top sabotage; Security Keycard before the Secondary Fermentation sprint; grape knife before Harvest Season and Grapes of Wrath; safe file before Valentina; microphone and voltage before the torch; car keys before the car; boat key before the boat; Tactical Wet Suit before the cave.
  • No NPC removed before they're needed. Vargas survives until after the tour clue fires (Run 1). The silo sniper team survives on the Eyes On Target branch (Melon Head and the kill order both need them). Kenneth Flowers is only taken after the wine-fetch line. Valentina is left alive in Run 3 and Run 4.
  • No scripted event broken by ordering. Both targets are alive when the tour ends in Run 1, so Closing Statement triggers. Both are alive when the Heralds sit down. The lawyer is given time to walk to the Guest Room before Run 4 goes there.
  • Save points don't strand objectives. Every branch reloads to a save taken before the branch's irreversible action, and the run's final branch is the one that exits.
  • Persistent progress used deliberately. Chameleon, Discover Mendoza and the three shortcuts all track across playthroughs, so they are spread across runs rather than crammed into one. Run 1 opens the two basement shortcuts first, which shortens Runs 3–6.
  • Backtracking minimised. Run 1 ends in the Villa Bedroom, which is where the car keys are — so Grand Theft Auto costs nothing. Run 2 stays inside the winery. Run 3 works west to east, gardens up into the villa basement. Run 4 works bottom-up: Cinema → Asado → Villa. Run 5 handles the four remaining exits from one save.
  • Coverage. All 61 challenges are assigned to at least one run; no duplicates in the assignment; the Checklist tab and the run assignments both total 61.

Three facts that change how you route this mission

1. No Mastery, no Mission Stories, one start. Patient Zero has no Mastery track, so nothing is gated behind a level, there is only one starting location, and there are no Mission Stories to order or break. Since Update 3.150 you can pick any starting suit — but suits, the Recon Gear and the VIP Patient outfit all make the entire map hostile.

2. Seven of the 18 are Hokkaido-wide, not Patient Zero-specific. The five Versatile challenges plus Versatile Assassin, Chameleon and Discover Hokkaido all read "in Hokkaido" in their in-game text. They appear on Patient Zero's list but count from any Hokkaido mission — which matters a lot, because two of them are far easier in Situs Inversus.

3. Challenges bank the moment they pop. Anything that does not say "complete the mission" unlocks immediately and survives a reload. That is why Run 02 is a single entry with five save-scummed kills instead of five separate runs.

The infection mechanic decides almost everything

Touching Owen Cage or any infected NPC without the Bio Suit infects 47, starting a five-minute death timer, and you must use the antidote before leaving. That single rule is why melee challenges go on Liebleid and not Cage, why Sniper Assassin needs containment first, and why the Bio Suit permanently kills No Rubber.

Two corrections to the public wiki

"No Evidence" is not in the current challenge list. The Hitman Wiki lists it as a Patient Zero assassination challenge, a 2017 French Steam guide lists it, and a well-known video is titled "7 Challenges in 1" — which only reaches seven if No Evidence is counted. It is absent from the mission data. Most likely it was removed or folded in at some point. If your game does show it, Run 01 earns it for free, since that route already finishes with no bodies.

"Nothing but Clear KAIs" is not one of the 18. The wiki's challenges subpage lists it under Patient Zero. It is a Hokkaido location challenge from the Cloud Nine pack (HITMAN III, Year 3 winter) and does not appear in this mission's data. Worth doing for the White Sunset suit, but it is not part of this 18.

Chameleon caveats

Wording conflict. The in-game text reads "find and equip all disguises in Hokkaido", but the data attaches a 16-item list specific to Patient Zero, and community guides publish separate per-mission lists. Treat it as this mission's 16, and if it does not pop, check whether a Situs Inversus-only outfit is also being demanded.

Known bug. A Steam thread reports Chameleon failing to register on Patient Zero after all sixteen were worn. Whether it was ever patched is UNVERIFIED. Mitigation from that thread: start fresh rather than from a mid-mission reload, and if you began in a disguise, switch out of it and back in.

Progress reset bug. A separate thread reports the Classics/Versatile set spontaneously resetting in Sapienza and Hokkaido. Screenshot the challenge screen after each pop.

You Know the Number — wording conflict

Three sources describe this challenge three ways:

  • Wiki challenge article: witness at least 47 concurrent infections, complete all objectives, escape — and notes that killing victims as they become infected can make it unachievable, while total kill count does not matter.
  • Wiki mission table: kill at least 47 people infected with the virus besides Owen Cage.
  • Steam redacted-challenges guide: eliminate at least 47 infected people.

Route that satisfies all three readings: let the count reach 47 or more live infected, then kill them all, then escape. Run 04 is built that way.

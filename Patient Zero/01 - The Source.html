Four things that shape every run below

  • Play on Professional. The Sniper Assassin tile explicitly requires Professional difficulty. Master limits saves, which would break the reload runs; Casual won't award the tile.
  • No Mission Stories exist in this mission (Hitman Wiki), so there is no story order to protect and no "Hero of Bangkok" tile. Nothing here depends on story triggers.
  • Challenges bank the instant they pop. Reloading or quitting does not take them back, which is what makes the save-scum runs work.
  • Starting locations are unverified. Your export lists disguises and challenges but no starting locations, and no source consulted documents any for The Source. Each loadout card says so; read your own planning screen.

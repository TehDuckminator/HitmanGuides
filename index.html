Progress is read live from each guide's own checklist and is stored in this browser only. Tick challenges inside a guide — this page updates the moment you come back to it. A card turns green on its own once every challenge in its guide is done.

For the totals to add up across guides, all the pages must share one browser storage. Opening them over Start Guides.cmd (http://localhost) guarantees that in every browser; opening the files directly works in Edge and Chrome but not Firefox.

Segment size is that guide's share of all 1,544 tracked challenges. Cards without a link have no guide written yet and are not counted. Another Life tracks its 35 route steps rather than a challenge list; every other guide tracks challenges. Each guide has its own reset button.

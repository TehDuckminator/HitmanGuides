    Three things this mission does not have

    No Mission Stories. Confirmed by the wiki and by your export. There is no story order to protect and no "complete all stories" challenge. Guides that list mission stories here are describing Another Life, the main Whittleton Creek mission.

    No Chameleon and no discovery areas. Your export reports disguiseCount: 0 and areaCount: 0, with areas shared from Another Life. There is no disguise set to collect and no map to sweep.

    Eight kill methods, one target. Every kill challenge is mutually exclusive within a single life, so Runs 4 and 5 branch off one save each. Run the runs in order — Versatile Assassin in Run 5 needs challenges banked in Runs 1 and 4.

    Corrections since the previous version

    • Someone Could Hurt Themselves moved off the mailbox bomb. A planted remote explosive registers as an explosion kill, and no source confirms it also counts as an accident. It now sits on the electrocution in Run 4, which is unambiguously an accident, with the fire kill in the same run as a second chance.
    • Flammable Snake Oil now has a real method. The challenge page gives it: shoot the red barrel to start an oil leak, clear Vholes' personal guard, then coin-lure him into the puddle during his outdoor phone call and shoot the oil. The previous "carry his body to the barrel" was an assumption and is gone.
    • Letterboxed and Big Bang Theory now have their trigger. Both pages say the same thing: place the item, wait for Vholes to answer the door once the sheriff rings it, detonate as he approaches the mailbox. Previously flagged unverified; now confirmed twice.
    • I Am the Law now has a specific NPC and moment. The challenge page names the single outdoor deputy who examines the garden fence once the police begin protecting the house. Body goes in the nearby bush.
    • Cellar Door no longer requires the aide pacifications. The challenge fires on the shutdown itself. Pacifying the two investigating aides only matters if you need the alarm to stay down, which nothing else in Run 3 does — so Run 3 skips it and leaves the basement immediately, which also keeps bodies away from the treehouse sequence.
    • Run 3 reordered into a single loop. Previously the treehouse came first "for safety". Cellar Door's own page says to move fast before the deputies enter the building, and I Am the Law needs the deputies to have deployed — so basement first, treehouse while the police arrive, then the fence deputy, then the mailbox. No backtracking.

      Source conflicts, unresolved

      • Which fence you vault. The wiki walkthrough says vault the fence on the left side of the house and drop to the basement. The Steam speedrun route says jump the fence on the right to reach the gardeners. Both reach the same rear garden; each run uses the wording of the source it came from.
      • How the alarm is disabled. The in-game intelligence entry says the lasergrid is disabled by tripping the main breaker in the basement. The walkthrough and the Cellar Door page both describe the keycard-locked vault instead. Both are in the basement. The route uses the vault because two sources describe it step by step.
      • Challenge count listed as 23. The wiki's category page cross-lists Ectoplasmic and No Evidence, both cut in HITMAN III, plus Cloud Nine and Welcome to Business Class, which belong to Another Life. Your export and the wiki mission table both say 21.
      • Challenge text wording. Your export's Silent Assassin and Sniper Assassin entries omit the "erase or destroy camera evidence" clause that the wiki table includes. The checklist quotes your export, since that is the in-game text.
